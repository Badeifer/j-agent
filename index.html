J-Agent

J-Agent is a personal desktop tool used by one person, its developer. It runs on his own computer and is not offered to other users.

What it does

With his permission it reads his Gmail messages (read only) and sorts them into categories he defines, saves email drafts he asks for, shows his Google Calendar next to his notes, and keeps a small set of reminders on one calendar he names.

It never sends email, never labels or deletes email, and never changes calendar events it did not create. Results are stored on his computer.
