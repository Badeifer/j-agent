J-Agent

J-Agent is a personal desktop tool used by one person, its developer. It runs on his own computer and is not offered to other users.

What it does

With his permission it reads his Gmail messages (read only) and sorts them into categories he defines, and it reads his Google Calendar (read only) to show his upcoming events next to his notes.

It never sends, changes or deletes email, and never creates, changes or deletes calendar events. Everything it reads stays on his computer.
